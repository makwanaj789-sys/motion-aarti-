# Song text used on screen (Devanagari)

Song: "Shubhaarambh" (Kai Po Che!, music by Amit Trivedi, lyrics by Swanand Kirkire).
Words come from the reference reel's own lyric captions, which are timed to the
vocals, transliterated into Devanagari. The word "डोली" was read from the
caption frames at 12.1–12.3 s, where the deity image only partly covers it.

| Reference time (s) | Text |
|---|---|
| ~2.0 – 4.0 | शुभारंभ |
| ~7.8 – 10.0 | हो शुभारंभ · हे शुभारंभ |
| ~10.0 – 12.0 | मंगल बेला आए |
| ~12.0 – 14.0 | सपनों की डोली पे दिल की |
| ~14.0 – 15.6 | बाजी रे शहनाई |

Fonts, all SIL OFL:
- Teko 700: bold condensed display. Drawn condensed, not stretched.
- Tiro Devanagari Hindi: elegant serif for the longer phrases.
- Kalam 700: handwritten, for a few short accents only.

All three were checked for correct matra, anusvara and conjunct shaping.
